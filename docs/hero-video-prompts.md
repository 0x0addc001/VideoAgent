# VideoAgent Hero Video Prompts

These prompts are written for Kling `text_to_video` with
`kling-video-v4_0`, `9:16`, `720p`, and a 5–6 second duration. The final clips
can be cropped to the 3:4 hero cards.

## Diversity Matrix

| Detail | European Creator | Black Creator | East Asian Creator |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Environment | Coastal Copenhagen rooftop, overcast daylight | Sunlit independent record shop | Dark creative studio |
| Primary action | Walking toward camera with a gimbal | Browsing vinyl and showing a record | Checking focus on a camera |
| Expression | Playful, knowing half-smile | Quiet laugh, curious glance back | Calm, confident, restrained |
| Wardrobe | Cobalt-blue weatherproof trench | Emerald-green satin bomber | Charcoal-and-ivory minimal outfit |
| Frame position | Left third, moving inward | Center, turning into profile | Right third, static placement |
| Camera language | Backward tracking at walking pace | Frontal handheld follow | Near-still 50mm portrait |

## 1. European Creator

```text
Premium cinematic campaign video for a modern AI video creation platform. One
continuous five-second shot on a contemporary coastal rooftop in Copenhagen
under soft overcast daylight. A fictional stylish Northern European female
content creator in her late twenties has fair skin with light freckles, grey-
green eyes, and auburn hair tied into a loose low ponytail. She films a personal
vlog with a compact mirrorless camera mounted on a small handheld gimbal.
Vertical 3:4 composition, subject positioned in the left third and walking
slowly inward toward the center, with the pale Nordic skyline and open sea
visible behind her.

She holds the gimbal at chest level, checks the framing on its screen, then
lifts her eyes directly toward the lens with a playful, knowing half-smile. Her
free hand briefly catches a loose strand of hair blown across her face. Natural
blinking, relaxed breathing, realistic hair and fabric motion. Exactly one
person and two natural hands. She wears an oversized cobalt-blue weatherproof
trench over a cream ribbed top, small silver earrings, and minimal natural
makeup. Authentic skin texture, visible freckles, no beauty-filter appearance.

Cool pale-blue daylight, concrete and glass rooftop architecture, soft
seafoam-grey clouds, distant blurred city silhouettes, and a gentle coastal
breeze. The cobalt coat creates a restrained color accent against the cool
environment. Shallow depth of field, 35mm lens perspective, and a smooth
backward-tracking camera that moves at the same pace as her walk with a very
slight handheld breathing motion.

Single continuous take, no cuts, no zoom, no scene change. The beginning and
ending frame should have nearly the same subject placement and lighting so the
video loops naturally. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no user
interface, no duplicate person, no extra limbs, no distorted hands, no face
morphing, no plastic skin, no exaggerated smile, no fast movement, no busy
background.
```

## 2. Black Creator

```text
Premium cinematic campaign video for a modern AI video creation platform. One
continuous five-second shot inside a sunlit independent record shop in the
early afternoon. A fictional stylish Black female content creator in her early
thirties has deep brown skin, short sculpted natural hair, and warm brown eyes.
She films a personal vlog with a compact mirrorless camera mounted on a small
handheld gimbal. Vertical 3:4 composition, subject centered slightly left of
frame between narrow wooden record shelves, with warm window light falling
across the foreground.

While moving slowly along the shelves, she uses her free hand to pull out a
vinyl sleeve and angles it toward her vlog camera to show the viewer. She
laughs quietly with an amused, spontaneous expression, then turns over her
shoulder and glances back toward the lens with one eyebrow subtly raised.
Natural blinking, relaxed movement, realistic fabric motion. Exactly one person
and two natural hands. She wears an emerald-green satin bomber jacket over an
ivory T-shirt, vintage gold hoop earrings, and understated natural makeup.
Authentic skin texture, no beauty-filter appearance.

Warm amber sunlight, deep teal walls, tightly packed vinyl shelves, soft dust
motes in the air, and blurred album covers in the background. The green jacket
and gold jewelry stand out against the warm wood and teal environment. Shallow
depth of field, 28mm lens perspective, and a gentle frontal handheld follow that
shifts with her turn while keeping the camera visible in her hand.

Single continuous take, no cuts, no zoom, no scene change. The beginning and
ending frame should have nearly the same subject placement and lighting so the
video loops naturally. No text, no letters, no logo, no watermark, no user
interface, no duplicate person, no extra limbs, no distorted hands, no face
morphing, no plastic skin, no exaggerated smile, no fast movement, no busy
background.
```

## 3. East Asian Creator

```text
Premium cinematic campaign video for a modern AI video creation platform. One
continuous five-second shot in a sophisticated dark creative studio. A
fictional stylish East Asian female content creator in her late twenties is
filming a personal vlog with a compact mirrorless camera mounted on a small
handheld gimbal. Medium close-up, vertical 3:4 composition, subject positioned
toward the right third with clean negative space around the upper left.

She calmly checks the camera screen, makes a subtle focus adjustment with one
hand, then looks up toward the lens with a quiet, confident expression. Natural
blinking, restrained movement, realistic hair and fabric motion. Exactly one
person and two natural hands. Fashion-forward minimal charcoal and ivory
outfit, refined natural makeup, authentic skin texture with subtle pores, no
beauty-filter appearance.

Dark charcoal and deep teal environment, warm orange rim light moving subtly
across her hair and shoulder, soft practical light panels out of focus in the
background, gentle atmospheric haze and subtle lens reflection. Shallow depth
of field, 50mm portrait-lens perspective, slow almost-still camera with a very
slight handheld breathing motion. High-end fashion campaign cinematography,
realistic anatomy, polished editorial lighting.

Single continuous take, no cuts, no zoom, no scene change, no camera shake. The
beginning and ending frame should have nearly the same subject placement and
lighting so the video loops naturally. No text, no letters, no logo, no
watermark, no user interface, no duplicate person, no extra limbs, no distorted
hands, no face morphing, no plastic skin, no exaggerated smile, no fast
movement, no busy background.
```
