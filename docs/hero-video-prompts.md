# VideoAgent Hero Video Prompts

These prompts are written for Kling `text_to_video` with
`kling-video-v4_0`, `9:16`, `720p`, and a 5–6 second duration. The final clips
can be cropped to the 3:4 hero cards.

## 1. European Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of a confident French woman in her late twenties
with fair skin, expressive green eyes, and shoulder-length chestnut hair. She
wears a cream trench coat and walks briskly along a windswept Lisbon rooftop at
golden hour, carrying a compact cinema camera on a gimbal in one hand and
pushing her hair back with the other. The camera tracks beside her, then arcs
around as she turns toward the lens and smiles into the warm sun. The Atlantic
coast is visible behind her, fabric and hair move naturally in the wind, with
editorial fashion-film color grading, realistic skin texture, and shallow depth
of field. No text, logos, extra fingers, or distorted face.
```

## 2. Black Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of a stylish Black woman in her thirties with
deep brown skin and natural sculpted curls, wearing a sculptural burnt-orange
jacket. She moves through a rain-washed city street at night, filming herself
with a compact camera while walking backward, laughing and turning under
reflective neon signs. She spins once as a passing bus casts moving light
across her face, and the camera follows in a smooth handheld orbit before
settling into a close portrait. Rich cyan and amber reflections, visible
natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, dynamic environmental movement.
No text, logos, extra fingers, or distorted face.
```

## 3. East Asian Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of an elegant East Asian woman in her
twenties, filming herself with a compact camera in a moody creative studio.
Warm orange light and cool blue shadows shape her face while she adjusts the
gimbal and looks toward the lens. The camera drifts slowly from a profile
close-up to a three-quarter portrait. Refined editorial color grading, natural
skin texture, gentle motion, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no
extra fingers, no distorted face.
```
