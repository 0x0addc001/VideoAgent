# VideoAgent Hero Video Prompts

These prompts are written for Kling `text_to_video` with
`kling-video-v4_0`, `9:16`, `720p`, and a 5–6 second duration. The final clips
can be cropped to the 3:4 hero cards.

## 1. European Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of a confident European woman in her late
twenties with fair skin, expressive green eyes, and shoulder-length chestnut
hair. She films herself with a compact mirrorless camera on a gimbal while
walking across a quiet European rooftop at golden hour. Soft wind moves her
hair, warm sunlight outlines her face, and the camera performs a slow, elegant
arc from a close-up to a medium shot. Editorial fashion-film color grading,
natural skin texture, shallow depth of field, subtle handheld energy, no text,
no logos, no extra fingers, no distorted face.
```

## 2. Black Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of a stylish Black woman in her thirties with
deep brown skin and natural sculpted curls. She holds a small cinema camera on
a stabilizer inside a modern studio with warm amber practical lights and a
deep teal background. She turns toward the lens and offers a quiet, confident
smile while the camera circles slowly around her. Premium editorial lighting,
visible natural skin texture, soft rim light, shallow depth of field, elegant
handheld movement, no text, no logos, no extra fingers, no distorted face.
```

## 3. East Asian Creator

```text
Cinematic vertical vlog footage of an elegant East Asian woman in her
twenties, filming herself with a compact camera in a moody creative studio.
Warm orange light and cool blue shadows shape her face while she adjusts the
gimbal and looks toward the lens. The camera drifts slowly from a profile
close-up to a three-quarter portrait. Refined editorial color grading, natural
skin texture, gentle motion, shallow depth of field, no text, no logos, no
extra fingers, no distorted face.
```
